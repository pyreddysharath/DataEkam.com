Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Your data stays yours. Everything you paste, upload, format, convert or compare on Data Ekam runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent to, stored on or logged by our servers.

What we do not collect

  • No file storage. Files you drop or attach are read locally by your browser and are never uploaded.
  • No content logging. Pasted text, formatted output, converted files and comparisons are not recorded or transmitted.
  • No accounts. The site has no sign-up, so we hold no names, email addresses or passwords for visitors.

What we do receive

Feedback you choose to send. If you use the feedback form, we receive the name and message you type, together with the date and time, and only when you press Send Feedback. Please do not include sensitive information in a feedback message.

Stored in your browser

The site saves one item in your browser's local storage: your cookie-consent choice (accepted or declined), so that the banner does not reappear on every visit. It stays on your device, and you can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Advertising and cookies

Data Ekam is free and is intended to be supported by advertising through Google. Where advertising is shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's earlier visits to this site or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • These cookies may be used for ad personalisation, frequency capping and conversion measurement.

We ask for your consent before any advertising cookies are set. If you decline, the site works in exactly the same way and you will not see personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie icon at the bottom of the main page.

You can also opt out of personalised advertising from Google in Google's Ads Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services the site loads

To display and run the site, your browser requests files from a small number of other providers. As with any web request, those providers can see your IP address and browser details:

  • Our hosting provider, which serves the pages and may keep standard access logs.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the Montserrat typeface.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare), which supplies the open-source libraries used for Excel, YAML and PDF handling.

None of these requests contain the data you paste or upload.

Links to other sites

The site links to our other projects and to external references. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

Data Ekam is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
